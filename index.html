Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before you inquire.

These answers describe the concept flow. Hernandez Concrete must confirm its exact service area, estimate process, contact details, and current project scope before launch.

What project types can I ask about?

Use the form to ask about driveways and concrete replacement, patios and pavers, foundations and ADU-related concrete, retaining walls, sidewalks, or curbs. Hernandez Concrete can confirm whether the project fits its current scope.

How do I know whether my location is served?

Enter the project address or city in your request. The exact service area must be confirmed for each location.

What information should I include?

Include the project type, location, approximate size, current condition, timing, possible removal needs, and any access or site constraints.

Can I send project photos?

Photo upload is shown as a proposed final-site feature. This static concept does not upload or store files.

What happens after I send the request?

Hernandez Concrete can review the information and confirm whether more details, photographs, plans, or an on-site look are needed. The exact process still requires confirmation.

Does requesting an estimate commit me to the project?

No. Sending a request starts a conversation and does not authorize work. Any estimate, scope, schedule, payment terms, or agreement must be confirmed separately.
